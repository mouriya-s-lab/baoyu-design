# Baoyu Whiteboard

Lays out diagrams, flows, maps of ideas, plans and comparisons on a whiteboard in your workspace and opens it in a board editor that runs on your computer. Move things, draw, add sticky notes and sections, comment for the agent, and share the board on your local network. Runs locally on Node.js 22 or later; nothing leaves your computer unless you share it.

Skills: `claude-whiteboard`.

It runs on your computer, in the ChatGPT desktop app or Codex, and needs Node.js 22 or later.

Built from [baoyu-design](https://github.com/JimLiu/baoyu-design); see [how to install it](https://github.com/JimLiu/baoyu-design/blob/main/chatgpt-plugins/README.md). Terms: [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md).
